Community Meeting Recap

Thanks to everyone who joined us online on October 28, and to our guest speakers. The highlights:

Our new park: The 6-acre neighborhood park at 2298 Delta View Ave. is on a tentative City schedule: amenities plan approved in spring 2027, construction starting spring 2028, and completion targeted for the end of 2028.

Police: Lt. Dan DeLeon is moving to Metro to oversee SWAT, K9, and EOD. Lt. Gerald Lambert takes over District 5A, which includes Delta Shores. Thank you, Lt. DeLeon!

Schools: Sac City Unified is working toward buying land south of the Cosumnes River for a possible future school. A new elementary school needs about 300 local students to be sustainable (about 25 to 30 today). John Still is set for a major modernization starting next summer.

Development: Quick Quack Car Wash (west of Chick-fil-A) was approved and projects an early-2028 opening; Raising Cane's is moving to construction permits; a BMO Bank branch is under review; and several new housing projects are moving forward.

City Hall: The Mayor's State of the City is November 3. Red-light cameras are returning to high-risk intersections ($200 civil fines). Youth violence prevention grants (about $1.5 million total) are open to nonprofits until November 3.

Association news: Please welcome Monserrat Garcia, our new Vice President! And congratulations to our Secretary/Treasurer, Daron Go, on his election as a resident director on the C&C at Delta Shores HOA board (Coronado and Catalina). As a reminder, the Delta Shores Community Association is a neighborhood nonprofit and is separate from any HOA.
