Welcome to Delta Shores!

Meet your community association: the neighbors who bring Delta Shores together and make sure our voice is heard.

The Delta Shores Community Association is a grassroots nonprofit formed by residents and local businesses who want a connected, engaged neighborhood. We're not an HOA. Your HOA takes care of maintenance and rules for your subdivision. We focus on everything else that makes a neighborhood feel like home: bringing neighbors together, and working with city leaders on what Delta Shores needs.

As Delta Shores grows, with new parks and a school planned, we're working to make sure residents help shape what comes next.

Neighborhood Association of the Year Recognized by Sacramento City Councilmember Mai Vang, District 8.

What we do

Community events

Block parties, Cinco de Mayo, Halloween, National Night Out and the Delta Shores Festival of Lights.

A voice at City Hall

We've hosted the Mayor, our Councilmember, our Assemblymember, police and fire leaders to talk about Delta Shores' needs.

Community meetings

Regular neighborhood meetings, in person and online, with updates on development, parks and safety.

Neighborhood Watch

Neighbors looking out for each other, sharing information and staying aware.

Parks & beautification

Surveying residents and advocating for the new Delta Shores park and green spaces.

Local business perks

Our Business Spotlight features nearby businesses, with exclusive deals for residents.
