We are incredibly proud to announce that the Delta Shores Community Association has been officially recognized as the Neighborhood Association of the Year!

  • This prestigious recognition was awarded to us by Sacramento City Councilmember Mai Vang of District 8, acknowledging the hard work and dedication of our entire neighborhood.

  • As highlighted during the award presentation, our association is a "vibrant, forward-thinking organization dedicated to fostering a connected, safe, and beautifully maintained South Sacramento".

  • Because we serve one of the area's premier master-planned communities, we are committed to working tirelessly to "preserve neighborhood quality while building strong community connections".

  • The award plaque and recognition reflect our core mission: "Founded by residents, the association advocates for homeowners, partners with local businesses to support regional economic growth, and brings neighbors together through family-friendly events and activities".

  • Located near premier retail, dining, and the Sacramento River corridor, we are honored that Delta Shores "continues to help shape the future of South Sacramento alongside District 8".

  • A huge thank you to all our residents and board members for making this possible!
