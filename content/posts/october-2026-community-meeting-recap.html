Thank you to everyone who joined our online Community Meeting on Wednesday, October 28, and to our guest speakers for taking the time to update Delta Shores. Here are the highlights.

Take these surveys

  • Delta Shores Park Survey: help decide what goes in our new park. Closes November 6.

  • Police Chief Recruitment Community Survey: tell the City Manager what you want in Sacramento's next Police Chief. Closes November 5.

Our new neighborhood park

The 6-acre neighborhood park will be at 2298 Delta View Avenue, at Delta View Avenue and Tidal View Way. As a neighborhood park, it isn't planned to have restrooms. The tentative City schedule shared at the meeting:

  • Park amenities plan reviewed and approved: spring 2027

  • Construction documents prepared: 2027

  • Project goes out to bid: winter 2027

  • Construction begins: spring 2028

  • Targeted completion: end of 2028

Your survey answers will shape the design, so please take it and share it with your neighbors.

Police update

Lieutenant Dan DeLeon, the geographical lieutenant for District 5A (which includes Delta Shores), is moving to Metro to oversee the SWAT, K9, and EOD teams. Lieutenant Gerald Lambert will take over the district. Thank you, Lieutenant DeLeon, for your service to our community.

Schools

Sacramento City Unified School District Trustee Chinua Rhodes and Facilities head Chris Ralston shared that the district is working through state approval and soil testing to buy land south of the Cosumnes River for a possible future school. Today only about 25 to 30 district students live in Delta Shores, and a new elementary school needs roughly 300 to be sustainable; the district projects about 500 over the next 10 years as homes are built. John Still is also set for a major modernization starting next summer, including expanded elementary wings and upgraded playfields.

Development updates

  • Quick Quack Car Wash (west of Chick-fil-A): unanimously approved by the Planning Commission on October 8. Permits are being submitted now; opening is projected for early 2028.

  • Raising Cane's: approved and moving into the construction-permit phase.

  • BMO Bank: a 3,900 sq. ft. branch with drive-thru is under review.

  • Delta Shores East at 24th Street: tentative map under review to divide 58.3 acres into 533 parcels.

  • Delta Shores Circle at Watershed: 344 multi-family units planned on 12.8 acres.

  • Villages at Delta Shores East: 239 single-family homes on 52.2 acres.

  • A 210-lot single-family subdivision at Cosumnes River Blvd. North was approved in July.

From the Mayor's Office

The Mayor's State of the City is on November 3. The City is also restarting its automated red-light camera program at high-risk intersections, with $200 civil fines tied to license plates (not driving records); revenue goes to traffic safety and road maintenance. We also raised concerns about sideshows and youth fights at the shopping center, and the Mayor's Office will follow up with the Police Department.

From the District 8 Council Office

  • Youth violence prevention grants: about $1.5 million is available for nonprofits serving youth and young adults, including Gang Prevention and Intervention grants of up to $200,000. Applications are due November 3.

  • Get ready for winter storms: with El Niño expected, put together an emergency kit, make a flood evacuation plan, and never drive through flood water. Check your home's flood risk with the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and the City of Sacramento flood maps.

Association news

Please welcome Monserrat Garcia, our new Vice President! And congratulations to our Secretary/Treasurer, Daron Go, on his election as a resident director on the C&C at Delta Shores HOA board, which covers the Coronado and Catalina neighborhoods. As a reminder, the Delta Shores Community Association is separate from any HOA: we're a neighborhood nonprofit that connects all Delta Shores residents and businesses with each other and with civic leaders.
