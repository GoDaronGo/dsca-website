Tuesday, December 1, is #GivingTuesday, a worldwide day of generosity when people come together to support the causes close to home. This year, we're asking you to invest in the one that's right outside your front door: Delta Shores.

Look what we've done together

The Delta Shores Community Association is led by your neighbors. Together, we've:

  • Thrown block parties for Cinco de Mayo and Halloween, and joined National Night Out

  • Brought the Mayor, our Councilmember, police and school leaders to Delta Shores to hear directly from residents

  • Surveyed neighbors about our new park, so residents help decide what gets built

  • Organized a Neighborhood Watch and launched a Business Spotlight with exclusive deals for residents

  • Been named Neighborhood Association of the Year by Councilmember Mai Vang

  • Planned the first Delta Shores Festival of Lights, lighting up Flowing Way on December 5

Why we're asking

Until now, our work has been made possible by our partners: SMUD, our lead sponsor since our founding, the City of Sacramento, and local businesses like Casa Flores, which opens its doors for our meetings. We're grateful to every one of them.

But Delta Shores is growing fast, with new homes, a new park and a future school on the way, and we want to do more. More events. More ways for neighbors to connect. A stronger voice when decisions are made about our community. To get there, we need the people who live here to help carry it forward.

A small gift from each household adds up quickly, and it means our community is funded by our community.
