Big news for Delta Shores: the City of Sacramento is designing a brand-new 6-acre neighborhood park right here in our community, and they want to hear from residents before the plans are drawn.

Delta Shores Park P-9 will be located at 2298 Delta View Ave., at the corner of Delta View Ave. and Tidal View Way. The City's Youth, Parks & Community Enrichment Department is running a short community survey (about 5 to 8 minutes) to learn which features, amenities, and layout matter most to you.

Take the survey by November 6

Take the Delta Shores Park P-9 Survey

The survey asks about things like:

  • Who in your household will use the park and how you'll get there

  • Your favorite playground theme (clubhouse, river, or farm)

  • Your top 5 amenities, such as playgrounds, picnic areas, sports courts, walking paths, community gardens, and more

  • Whether the park should host community events like movie nights or farmers' markets

  • Ideas for the park's name

Prefer paper? You can pick up and return a paper copy at Pannell Meadowview Community Center, 2450 Meadowview Rd.

Good to know

This is a neighborhood park, designed to serve residents within about a half-mile. Neighborhood parks typically don't include features found in larger community or regional parks, such as lighted sports fields, restrooms, community centers, pools, parking lots, or dog parks.

The City plans to post survey results on the Delta Shores Park P-9 webpage by mid-November. The City's landscape architect also plans to share the draft site plan with residents at a Delta Shores Community Association meeting in early 2027, before it goes to the Parks Commission and City Council.
