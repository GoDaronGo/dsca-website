Winter storm season is here, and it can bring heavy rain, strong winds and flooding in some areas. The City of Sacramento has put together resources to help residents get ready, and asked us to pass them along. A few minutes of prep now can save you a soggy garage or a long night without power later.

Sign up for Sacramento Alert

The most important step: register for Sacramento Alert to get emergency notifications from the City sent straight to your phone. It only takes a few minutes.

Before a storm

  • Put together an emergency kit and keep it somewhere easy to grab: flashlights, batteries, water, snacks, medications and a phone charger.

  • Charge your phones and devices when a storm is in the forecast, in case the power goes out.

  • Store or tie down anything outside that wind can pick up: patio furniture, trash bins, umbrellas and holiday inflatables and decorations.

  • Clear your gutters and downspouts, and sweep leaves away from the storm drain in front of your home so water can drain.

  • Hold your leaves on the lawn. If a storm is coming, the City asks that you keep leaf piles out of the street until closer to your Claw pickup day, so they don't block drains. (Our leaf season post has the details.)

  • Get sandbags ahead of time if your home is prone to water problems. The City only hands them out when flooding is imminent and an emergency is declared.

  • Make a plan to stay in touch with your family if you get separated or phones go down.

Look out for your neighbors

Check in on neighbors who live alone, older residents and anyone who might need a hand, before and after a storm. And bring your pets inside, out of the weather.

Who to call

  • 311 or (916) 264-5011 (or use the Sac311 app or website): street flooding, fallen trees or limbs, plugged storm drains, traffic signals or streetlights out, and downed street signs. Save non-urgent, non-storm calls until after the storm passes.

  • SMUD power outages: 1-888-456-7683

  • 911 for life-threatening emergencies, including downed power lines. Stay well away from them.
