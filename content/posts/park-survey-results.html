Thank you to everyone who took the City's survey for our new 6-acre neighborhood park at Delta View Avenue and Tidal View Way. The survey closed November 6, and the City has posted the results.

See the full survey results (PDF)

Top amenities

Neighbors could pick up to five amenities. Walking paths were the clear favorite, chosen by 83% of respondents, followed by a children's playground (66%) and a group picnic area (59%). Outdoor fitness equipment (39%) and a native/pollinator garden (36%) rounded out the top five.

Playground theme

It was close. The River theme edged out the Clubhouse theme, 44% to 40%, with the Farm theme at 12%.

Naming the park

Landscape features drew the most votes as a naming theme (39%), ahead of cultural or community identity (24%), native plants or animals (22%), and Native American heritage (13%).

Neighbors also offered 56 name suggestions. More than half included "Delta," with Delta Shores Park, Delta Shores Community Park, and Delta Breeze Park each coming up several times.

What else neighbors said

In the open comments, the most common themes were restrooms, a dog area, parking, shade trees, splash pads, good lighting, and keeping the park safe and clean. Several neighbors also hoped the nearby pond could be part of the design.

Keep in mind that neighborhood parks like this one typically don't include restrooms, parking lots, or dog parks, so some of these requests may not fit the City's plans.

What happens next

The City's proposed schedule:

  • February 2027: the park amenities plan comes to a Delta Shores Community Association meeting for review

  • Spring 2027: City Council reviews the park name and amenities plan

  • 2027: construction documents prepared, with bidding in winter 2027

  • Spring 2028: construction begins, with completion targeted for the end of 2028

We'll share the February meeting date as soon as it's set. Join our mailing list for updates, and visit the City's Delta Shores Park P-9 webpage for the latest.
