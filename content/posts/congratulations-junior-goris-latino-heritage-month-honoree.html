Please join us in congratulating our President and co-founder, Jose "Junior" Goris! Assemblymember Stephanie Nguyen named Junior one of her 2026 Latino Heritage Month Honorees for Assembly District 10, and he was recognized on the floor of the California State Assembly on Monday, October 26.

The certificate honors Junior's leadership, service, and dedication to uplifting others and advancing the well-being of our community.

Since moving to Sacramento in 2011, Junior has championed grassroots, neighbor-to-neighbor engagement. Beyond leading the Delta Shores Community Association, he serves as a commissioner for the City of Sacramento and on the Sacramento County Children's Coalition Committee. Many of you know him from our block parties and community meetings, and from the countless hours he puts into connecting Delta Shores residents with city leaders.

Thank you, Junior, for everything you do for Delta Shores. Felicidades!
