A reminder for homeowners in the Coronado and Catalina neighborhoods: the C and C at Delta Shores Owners Association board meets on Monday, December 7, at 5:00 PM. The meeting will be held online.

How to join

The Helsing Group, the HOA's management company, emailed the meeting notice and agenda to owners on November 6. The details for joining are in that notice.

If you're a C&C homeowner and didn't receive it, contact Helsing at ccdeltashores@helsing.com.

Not sure if this is your HOA?

The C&C at Delta Shores HOA covers the Coronado and Catalina neighborhoods. The Delta Shores Community Association is a separate neighborhood nonprofit that serves all Delta Shores residents and businesses. We're sharing this reminder so C&C neighbors don't miss the meeting.
