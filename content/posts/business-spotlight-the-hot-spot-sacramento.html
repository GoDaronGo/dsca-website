Our Business Spotlight this month goes to The Hot Spot Sacramento, a locally owned, family-operated DIY creative studio right here in the Delta Shores Shopping Center, next to the movie theater. It's a place for creativity, laughter and making memories, and they've put together a special perk just for Delta Shores residents.

Delta Shores resident perk: 15% off walk-in crafts

Just mention "SHORE PERKS" when you visit.

Offer valid for Delta Shores residents on walk-in crafts only. Excludes parties, special events and other promotions.

Something fun for everyone

Planning a family outing, a date night, a girls' day or a birthday celebration? Or just looking for something fun to do? Here's a taste of what you can make at The Hot Spot:

  • Paint-your-own pottery

  • Custom candle making

  • Canvas painting

  • Mosaic art

  • Succulent and fairy gardens

  • Slime making

And the best part:

  • No studio fees

  • No experience needed

  • Walk-ins always welcome

  • Outside sips and bites welcome

Parties and group events

The Hot Spot also hosts birthday parties, private celebrations, team-building events, field trips and group experiences.
