Something new is coming to Delta Shores this holiday season. On Saturday, December 5, at 5:00 PM, we'll flip the switch on strands of lights stretched across Flowing Way, house to house over the street, and the whole neighborhood is invited.

The lighting ceremony

Join us on Flowing Way at 5:00 PM as the lights come on for the first time. We're expecting civic leaders from the Mayor's office and Councilmember Mai Vang's office, along with local news media and our sponsors. Kaiser Permanente will have an information table.

Led by Trustee Chinua Rhodes

The volunteer effort behind the Festival of Lights is being led by Chinua Rhodes, who represents our area (Trustee Area 5) on the Sacramento City Unified School District Board of Education. Trustee Rhodes grew up in South Sacramento and is a graduate of Sac City schools. Thank you, Trustee Rhodes, for bringing this to Delta Shores!

Come out and meet your neighbors

After the lights come on, stay a while. Stroll the street, enjoy some cocoa and cookies, and get to know the people who live around you. Come on foot if you can, and bring the family.

If you live on Flowing Way

Lights and decorations are provided at no cost to participating homes, and volunteers will put them up for you. If you haven't signed up yet, or you have questions, email info@deltashorescommunity.com.

Thank you to our sponsors

The Delta Shores Festival of Lights is co-sponsored by the Delta Shores Community Association, SMUD, SEIU, and Kaiser Permanente.
