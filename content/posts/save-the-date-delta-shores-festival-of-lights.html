Something new is coming to Delta Shores this holiday season. On Saturday, December 5, strands of lights will go up across Flowing Way, stretched house to house over the street, and the whole neighborhood is invited to watch us flip the switch.

The plan for the day

  • Noon: 50 to 70 volunteers start hanging lights across the street and putting up lights and decorations in front yards.

  • About 5 PM, at dark: the lighting ceremony. We'll turn the lights on together.

  • Right after: stroll the street, enjoy cookies and treats set out by Flowing Way neighbors, and meet the people who live around you. Hot chocolate is in the works.

Who's joining us

We're expecting civic leaders from the Mayor's office and Councilmember Mai Vang's office, along with local news media and our sponsors. Kaiser Permanente will have an information table.

Where

The lights will be on Flowing Way. Come on foot if you can, and bring the family.

If you live on Flowing Way

Everything is provided at no cost to participating homes: your share of the LED strand that crosses the street, the hook at your roof peak, an extension cord and a timer if you need one, yard lights and decorations, and the crew to put it all up. You can work with the volunteers to arrange your yard the way you like it. We're asking participating homes to light up every evening in December.

If you haven't signed up yet, or you have questions, email info@deltashorescommunity.com.

Thank you to our sponsors

The Delta Shores Festival of Lights is co-sponsored by the Delta Shores Community Association, SMUD, SEIU, and Kaiser Permanente.
