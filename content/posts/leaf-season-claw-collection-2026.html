Fall is here, and the City of Sacramento's leaf season starts Monday, November 2. From that date, yard waste that doesn't fit in your green organics container can be raked into a pile in the street, where the City's "Claw" crews will scoop it up and take it to be composted.

Our trees in Delta Shores are still young, so most of us won't have big piles. But if you do end up with extra leaves or trimmings, this is an easy way to get rid of them.

Our first pickup: Monday or Tuesday, November 2–3

The first Claw pass through Delta Shores is expected on Monday or Tuesday, November 2–3. If you have debris to set out, put it at the curb by Sunday evening so it's ready when the crew comes through. The City updates its schedule daily and dates can shift (stormy weather slows things down), so check the Collection Calendar for your address to confirm your next pickup.

How to set out your pile

  • Fill your green organics container first. Weekly container pickup continues all season and usually comes more often than the Claw, so only pile what won't fit, and refill your container from the pile each week.

  • Keep it to 4 × 4 × 9 feet or smaller.

  • Leave room for the Claw. Keep cars, bins and anything else clear of the pile. If the Claw can't reach it, it won't be picked up, so it helps to avoid parking next to it.

  • Keep piles out of storm drains and bike lanes when you can.

Who gets leaf-season service

Not every home qualifies. Homes on a private street, or without a curb and gutter, don't receive Claw service, and apartment buildings and other commercial properties that aren't City customers can't put out leaf piles. If you're not sure whether your address is covered, look it up on the Collection Calendar.

For more details, read the City's announcement on Sacramento City Express or visit SacRecycle.
