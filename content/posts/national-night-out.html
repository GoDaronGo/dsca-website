Join Us for National Night Out! It is time to come together with neighbors, local law enforcement, and community leaders for National Night Out! This annual community-building campaign promotes police-community partnerships and neighborhood camaraderie to make our neighborhoods safer, more caring places to live.

  • We will have food and a great opportunity to connect with the people who live right down the street.

  • Building a tight-knit community is our best defense against crime and our best tool for creating a wonderful living environment.

  • Please visit our official event page for the location details.
