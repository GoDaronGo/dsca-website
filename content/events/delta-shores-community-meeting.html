Delta Shores Community
End-of-Year Meeting!

You’re Invited!

Join your neighbors for the Delta Shores Community Meeting—the last one of 2025!

  • 🗓️ Date: Wednesday, December 10th, 2025

  • ⏰ Time: 6:00 PM

  • 📍 Location: Casa Flores Mexican Restaurant

Enjoy some delicious food and great conversation as we wrap up the year. This is one not to miss!

  • The City’s Department of Community Response will come and talk about homelessness in the area, including how and what to report.

  • Meet new neighbors and connect with the community.

  • Hear important updates directly from your community and civic leaders.

All Delta Shores residents are welcome! We look forward to seeing you there.
