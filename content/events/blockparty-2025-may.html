You're Invited to a Neighborhood Block Party!

Come join your neighbors for some fun and community spirit at the west end of Flowing Way! It's the perfect chance to say hello, meet new friends, and catch up with familiar faces. We'll have food, drinks, a bounce house for entertainment, and of course, the company of our amazing neighbors!

This is a potluck-style gathering. If you'd like to bring something to share, popular ideas include Mexican dishes, finger foods, chips & salsa, sodas, or your favorite margarita (or other beverage). Contributions are welcome but definitely not required – just bring yourselves!

Huge thanks to our hosts, Yuri and Gloria on Flowing Way! If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to them directly, use the contact form below, or email events@deltashorescommunity.com.

We hope you can make it!
