Legal disclaimer

The Delta Shores Community Association (DSCA) is an independent, voluntary civic organization dedicated to fostering community engagement, hosting events, and connecting residents with local leaders across the greater Delta Shores area. The DSCA is not a Homeowners Association (HOA) and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or legally connected to any local HOA board, property management company, or real estate developer. The DSCA has no legal authority to collect assessments, manage common areas, or enforce community CC&Rs.
